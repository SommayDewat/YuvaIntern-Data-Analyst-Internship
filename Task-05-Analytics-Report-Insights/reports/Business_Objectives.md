
# Business Objectives — Sample Superstore Analytics

## Business Problem
The business needs to understand its sales performance,
profitability, product performance, regional performance,
and discount-related risks.

## Primary Objectives
1. Evaluate overall sales and profit performance.
2. Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
3. Compare regional sales and profitability.
4. Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
5. Understand the relationship between discounts and profit.
6. Identify products and sub-categories requiring attention.

## Key Business Questions
- Which category generates the highest sales?
- Which category generates the highest profit?
- Which region performs best?
- Which months have the highest sales?
- How do discounts relate to profitability?
- Which sub-categories generate negative profits?

## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Average Discount

## Stakeholders
- Business Management
- Sales Managers
- Regional Managers
- Product Managers

## Expected Outcome
A structured analytics report with visualizations,
business insights, executive summary, and actionable
recommendations.
